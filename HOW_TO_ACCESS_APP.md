## 📱 How to Use

### First Time Setup

1. **Open the website** in your browser
2. **Click "Register"** or "Sign Up"
3. **Create an account** with:
   - Email address
   - Password (at least 8 characters)
   - Name

### For Your Wife to Join

**Option 1: You invite her**
1. You log in to your account
2. Go to Account Settings or Profile
3. Click "Invite Spouse"
4. Enter her email address
5. She will receive an invitation
6. She creates her account and accepts the invitation

**Option 2: She creates her own account first**
1. She registers on the website
2. You send her an invitation from your account
3. She accepts the invitation
4. Now you both share the same budget data

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Can't access the website?
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Forgot password?
- Currently no password reset (coming soon)
- Contact the admin to reset manually

### Not seeing shared data?
- Make sure you've accepted the invitation
- Both users must be linked to the same shared account
- Try logging out and logging back in
