## 📱 How to Get Started

### Step 1: Open the Website
- Click the link above
- Or copy and paste it into your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)

### Step 2: Create Your Account
1. Click **"Register"** or **"Sign Up"**
2. Enter your:
   - Email address
   - Password (at least 8 characters)
   - Your name
3. Click **"Create Account"**

### Step 3: Connect Our Accounts
After you create your account, I will send you an invitation to share our budget. Once you accept, we'll both see the same:
- Expenses
- Budgets
- Credit cards
- Savings goals
- Income

## 📲 Use on Your Phone

### iPhone:
1. Open Safari
2. Go to the website link above
3. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow)
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Now you have an app icon on your phone!

### Android:
1. Open Chrome
2. Go to the website link above
3. Tap the **Menu** (three dots)
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Now you have an app icon on your phone!
